Why

Animal detection involves finding wildlife or pets in photos or videos. Traditional computer vision techniques like motion detection often caused false alarms and drained camera batteries. Deep learning has improved accuracy and also added the ability to classify animal species.

This is crucial for monitoring specific animals in the wild and alerting people to pests or predators — especially in farms and rural boundaries where fast response prevents damage.

The value of detecting animals

False alarms in security and surveillance systems caused by animals are a common issue, especially when relying solely on motion detection. FarmGuard AI reduces false alarms to a minimum and avoids alarm fatigue for operators by detecting animals using deep learning or filtering existing alarms using an animal classifier.

What

Classifying animal species

Animal detectors identify the presence and location of animals in videos and can use another AI model to classify detections into specific species or types. This enables tracking, recording, and census of specific animals while saving battery life and reducing data transfers.

Our AI models can be trained to recognize the animals that matter in your region — helping you detect predators, pests, or protected wildlife early.

Smart homes and invasive animals

The size of AI models aligns with the chipset and the accuracy vs performance needs of the application. Small models are efficient for low-power devices, while larger models offer higher accuracy and are optimized for powerful GPUs. FarmGuard AI lets you choose what fits your use case best.

Object size

Finding smaller individuals becomes increasingly challenging for a detector, but it also enables the use of lower resolution cameras or cheaper lenses. It’s a balancing act — our system helps you find the optimal strategy by allowing you to experiment with different settings and models.
